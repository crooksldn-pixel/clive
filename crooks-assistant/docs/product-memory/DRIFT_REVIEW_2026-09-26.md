# Drift Review - 2026-09-26

**Status:** the Director's review, at the owner's request, of whether the build is converging on CLIVE's philosophy or drifting from it. Measured against PRODUCT_BRAIN.md, CLIVE_INTENT_TO_EXECUTION_PHILOSOPHY.md (section 17, the north-star filter), CLIVE_ADAPTATION_AND_ORGANISATIONAL_INTELLIGENCE.md (section 13, the product and engineering filter) and CLIVE_SELF_KNOWLEDGE.md, at `clive/trunk` `e9d35b23`.

## 1. Verdict

The doctrine is intact and the recent product work points the right way; the execution has drifted. Roughly two weeks went into engineering machinery, while the owner's own day with CLIVE improved little: production still runs `ce791d03`, the voice has been off since 2026-09-22, and the operating-layer core the doctrine rests on (a persistent world, an event ledger, expectations, an objectives engine) is unbuilt. The machinery is now good enough. The correction is to point it at the owner's attention, and at CLIVE's ability to consume new capability, rather than at more machinery.

## 3. Where it drifts

1. **Machinery ahead of the north-star filter.** The filter asks whether a change reduced the remembering, checking, coordinating, translating, chasing or repeating the owner had to do. Most of the last fortnight reduced the owner's engineering chores, not his business ones. That was necessary once; it cannot stay the main line.
2. **The roadmap ran out of order.** Engineering organisation (roadmap D1-D11) was built before persistent business state (X2 World, X3 Event Ledger, X4 Expectations, X5 Attention, X7 Objectives). The external review of 2026-09-25 named the same pattern.
3. **Three approval systems and an empty ledger.** The actions engine, business objectives and the engineering kernel each carry their own propose, authorise, execute and verify cycle; the Judgment Ledger designed to hold the owner's authority records nothing. The durable kernel of PB 3.1 is not yet one kernel.
4. **The owner is still the courier at the edges.** Sudo steps, deploy pastes and landing sessions still route through him, and the Director acts only while he is in a chat. PB 2.3 wants him to be the exception handler.
5. **Stale device model.** PB 4.2 still describes CROOKS Control on a Mac and CROOKS Pad on a tablet. The reality is a phone, a production server and an engineering server. The record needs superseding, not deleting.
6. **Bespoke machinery at risk of becoming a cog in its own past (Adaptation 7-9).** The store, the loop and the bridge were built by hand. The Adaptation filter asks whether a superior technology could replace each without losing understanding, and whether CLIVE is protecting itself from new technology or learning to consume it. Today it has no way to consume it.
7. **Evaluation lags.** There is no replay gate, no continuous evaluation, and the two measures the owner agreed on 2026-09-25 (hours from ask to phone, minutes of owner attention per change) are not yet tracked.
8. **The operating layer is two systems wide.** Shopify and Gmail are the only live integrations; none of I1-I7 is built.

## 4. Corrections

1. **Finish and ship.** Deploy the trunk (the voice-health repair is in flight), restore voice, and track the two measures from the next change onwards.
2. **Build the Knowledge Digester next (KNOWLEDGE_DIGESTER_V1.md).** It answers drift 6 directly: CLIVE learns to consume the technology that would otherwise obsolete it, and every absorption stays reversible and provenance-tagged. Pointed at CLIVE's own repository and memory, the same machinery builds the self-knowledge the owner described.
3. **Then the operating-layer core, in the doctrine's order:** World and Event Ledger, Expectations, the attention home surface ("what needs me today", the Generative UI example of 2026-09-25) and the Objectives engine, all through one kernel with the ledger as the record of the owner's authority.
4. **Remove the remaining courier work:** the approved loop update part 2 (green-GitHub gate, protected safety core and loop code), automatic landing on green, and a continuous Director.
5. **Supersede the device model** in PRODUCT_BRAIN 4.2 with the phone, server and engineering-host reality.

## 5. The test going forward

Every change states which of these it serves: the owner's attention, CLIVE's capacity to absorb new capability, or the safety and provenance that make both trustworthy. A change that serves none of them is presentation or inertia.
