# CLIVE Self-Knowledge

**Status:** owner doctrine, stated 2026-09-24 and 2026-09-25. Philosophy, not an implementation claim.

## 1. The owner's words

- 2026-09-24: CLIVE is "a machine insisting upon itself". CLIVE is a byproduct of the processes that build it, and reflects, in a way, its own building process. Study the timeline of what it was and is to become, and check that the build is still going in the intended direction.
- 2026-09-25: the git product memory exists so that any future model understands the entire direction and philosophy, and so that eventually the product itself can understand itself and why it came to be. The owner believes this is a direction unlike anything heard of before.

## 2. What follows from it

1. **Nothing meaningful lives only in a conversation.** Every idea, direction, decision, rejection and lesson is written to product memory with its date, its source and its reasoning. The Director captures proactively; the owner should not have to say "log it".
2. **Provenance of intent.** Every capability should be traceable from the idea that asked for it, through the decision that approved it, the objective that built it and the review that accepted it, to the deploy that shipped it. That chain is what lets CLIVE answer "why do I do this" and "why was I built this way".
3. **The building process is part of the product.** The loop's objectives, reviews and integrations, the trunk's history and the owner's recorded judgments are CLIVE's own history, not scaffolding to discard.
4. **Honest history.** Memory records what happened, including mistakes (for example the red trunk of 2026-09-25 and the owner decision that was overtaken without a new one), not a cleaned-up narrative. A system that understands itself has to know where it went wrong.
5. **Understanding leads to proposing.** A CLIVE that can read its own memory can notice drift between intent and implementation, explain itself, and argue with evidence that one of its own decisions should be superseded (DIRECTOR_PROTOCOL supersession), always as a proposal the owner decides.

## 3. Not yet built

- A "why" index joining decisions, objectives, commits and deploys.
- CLIVE answering questions about itself from its own memory.
- The blind self-audit described in EXTERNAL_REVIEW_2026-09-25.md section 2.2, where expected answers are sealed outside anything CLIVE reads.
