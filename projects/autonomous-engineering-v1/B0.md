## The missing measurement (reported to George; nothing added)

**OAMAB's relay and final-evidence-review minutes have no existing source.** These are Termius, sudo,
copy-pasting errors, phone checks and reading reports. BASELINE_PLAN assigns them to PR 1's `--add`
entries. Without a record made at the time, they are lost, and B0's OAMAB would undercount them. V1
aims to remove exactly these minutes, so leaving them out would hide part of what V1 changes.

**Proposed, for his decision:** a hand-kept ledger in the format `engineering_measures --attention-log`
already reads, `{at, minutes, about}`. It would be written by the session he is in when a relay happens.
It needs no code, and nothing is kept until he agrees.

## Retention deadlines

- **Session transcripts on this host** are deleted 30 days after their last write (the default
  `cleanupPeriodDays`). Session minutes must be computed before that: by **2026-11-15** for the first
  B0 day.

  their timestamps are provided.
- The status and inbox branches keep full history (3,872 status snapshots since 25 Sep), and GitHub
  keeps Actions logs for 90 days by default. B0 needs only the runs' conclusions and times.
