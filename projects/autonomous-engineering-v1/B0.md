## When B0 freezes (George, 2026-10-16)

B0 freezes **only when both** of these hold:
1. **at least 7 full days have elapsed:** not before **2026-10-23 13:34:31 UTC**;
2. **at least 10 real engineering requests have reached a terminal state.**

**Terminal means:**
- COMPLETE;
- BLOCKED;
- OWNER_GATE, where the engineering attempt itself has ended and the request waits on George;
- refused, or otherwise terminal at intake, where relevant.

**Accepted builds are not required.** Failed and blocked work is part of what B0 measures.

**If 21 days pass** (by **2026-11-06 13:34:31 UTC**) with fewer than 10 terminal B0 requests, B0
freezes anyway. The smaller sample size, and the uncertainty it brings, is recorded explicitly with
every number.

The smoke request never counts towards the 10.

## OAMAB in B0: three sources, each minute counted once

**Counted:** only minutes in which George is actively doing something because of an engineering build.
**Not counted:**
- background waiting;
- model runtime;
- CI time;
- time he has walked away;
- V1 planning and measurement governance, including this planning session's discussion of B0 itself.

**Each activity has exactly one source:**

| Activity | Source | Minutes |
|---|---|---|
| A Director or supervisor session working on a B0 build | Its transcript: the timestamps of owner messages only | min(gap, 5) + 1 per message |
| His hold on a CLIVE card that files a B0 request | The filing record (the inbox commit) | 1, **replaced, not added to,** by a ledger `approval/hold` entry for the same request if he spent longer |
| Everything else: Termius and SSH, relays and copy-paste, diagnosis, reading evidence, clarifying a build with CLIVE, phone checks, and build actions taken in this planning session | **The manual ledger** | As recorded |

Activity inside a counted session is not also entered in the ledger. If it is by mistake, the overlap
in time is removed when OAMAB is computed: the total is the union of the intervals, not their sum.

Sessions whose transcripts are not visible from this host (for example on his Mac) count only through
the ledger.

## The manual owner-attention ledger (approved 2026-10-16; started 2026-10-16)

**File:** [`baseline/b0-attention.jsonl`](baseline/b0-attention.jsonl) on this branch, one JSON object
per line. It is a record only: no code, and no change to anything that runs.

**Who writes it:** the session George is in when the activity happens. If he was in no session, it is
written from what he reports.

**The fields:**
- `at`: UTC ISO time when the activity ended;
- `minutes`;
- `about`: the request or build id when known, otherwise `unattributed`;
- `request_id`: the id, or `null`;
- `category`;
- `reason`: short. **No customer data, secrets, hostnames or addresses;**
- `estimated`: `true` when the minutes are an estimate rather than timed;
- `source`: `"manual"`.

`at`, `minutes` and `about` are the format `engineering_measures --attention-log` already reads, and it
ignores the other fields.

**Categories** (a fixed set):
- `approval/hold`
- `relay/copy-paste`
- `SSH/Termius operation`
- `diagnosis/recovery`
- `evidence/review`
- `clarification/decision`
- `other`

**When exact timing is unavailable,** a reasonable estimate is recorded with `estimated: true`.
Precision is never invented.

## Retention deadlines

  `cleanupPeriodDays`).

  **2026-11-12**, whatever the freeze date.
